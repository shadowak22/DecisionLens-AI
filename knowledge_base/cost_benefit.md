# Cost-Benefit Analysis and Financial Decision Modeling

## 1. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)
Evaluating strategic initiatives requires holistic accounting beyond initial purchase prices or setup costs:
- **Capital Expenditure (CapEx)**: Upfront infrastructure, licenses, vendor setup fees, initial training workshops, and hardware procurement.
- **Operational Expenditure (OpEx)**: Recurring SaaS subscriptions, cloud egress, compute costs, API token consumption, continuous maintenance, vendor support tiers, and ongoing compliance audits.
- **Hidden & Indirect Costs**: Employee context switching, retraining overhead, legacy system decommissioning, productivity dips during onboarding, and integration middleware maintenance.

## 2. Return on Investment (ROI) and Payback Period
- **Net Present Value (NPV)**: Discounting future projected cash flows at the organization's Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) to ensure capital efficiency.
- **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**: The breakeven discount rate where NPV equals zero; projects exceeding hurdle rates (typically 12-18%) are prioritized.
- **Payback Velocity**: The time horizon required for cumulative net benefits to offset initial capital expenditure. Modern initiatives target 6 to 18 month payback periods.

## 3. Opportunity Cost and Sunk Cost Fallacy
- **Opportunity Cost**: Every resource allocated to Project A forfeits potential returns from Project B. Decisions must benchmark proposed initiatives against the highest-yielding alternative.
- **Sunk Cost Fallacy**: Past unrecoverable expenditures must be ignored in forward-looking decision models. Only marginal forward costs versus marginal forward benefits dictate strategic continuation or pivoting.

## 4. Financial Sensitivity and Scenario Modeling
- **Best Case (Optimistic)**: High adoption, minimal support overhead, strong efficiency gains (+30% upside).
- **Base Case (Realistic)**: Steady adoption curve, moderate implementation friction, standard maintenance costs.
- **Worst Case (Pessimistic)**: Prolonged rollout, unexpected licensing escalations, high integration churn, and user resistance (-20% to -50% downside).
